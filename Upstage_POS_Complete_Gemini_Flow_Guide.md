## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Workflow in Gemini Flow / AI Video Tool

1. **Folder Location**: Open `/Users/upstage_1/Desktop/Upstagekadai/video_assets/`. All 12 images (`00_logo.png` to `11_waiter_app.png`) are already saved and ready.
2. **Open Gemini Flow / Veo / Video Generator**:
   - Select **Image-to-Video (I2V)**.
   - Upload the image indicated for that scene (e.g. for Scene 2, upload `01_dashboard.png`).
3. **Copy & Paste Prompt**: Copy the exact prompt from this guide and paste it into the prompt box.
4. **Generate**: Click **Generate Video** (5 to 10 seconds clip per scene).
5. **Stitch in CapCut / Canva**:
   - Drag all 13 clips onto the timeline in order.
   - Add the Tamil voiceover (read aloud or use an AI voice).
   - Add Apple-style acoustic / chill piano background music at 10% volume.
   - Export in 1080p or 4K. Your professional Apple Support style app walkthrough video is ready!
